From warning to recovery

Warn

Early warnings about floods, drought and other hazards reach farmers in their area.

Fund

Farmers apply for a green micro-loan and receive it through M-Pesa.

Repay

Repayments go back through M-Pesa, so the cycle stays simple and traceable.

Built for phones without internet

USSD on *483#

Any phone can reach ResiliLink. Dial the code, pick from the menu, and get alerts or apply for a loan with no data and no app.

Green by design

Loans are meant for climate-smart choices that help farmers withstand the next shock, not only recover from the last one.
