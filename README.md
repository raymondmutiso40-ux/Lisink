# ResiliLink

Disaster early warnings and green micro-loans for farmers, reachable by M-Pesa and by USSD on any phone.

Built for the **IESS 2026** hackathon.

## The problem

Smallholder farmers are hit hardest by floods, drought and other climate shocks. Warnings often arrive late or not at all, and affordable finance to recover or adapt is hard to reach, especially without a smartphone or internet.

## What ResiliLink does

- **Early warnings:** sends disaster alerts to farmers in affected areas.
- **Green micro-loans:** offers small loans for climate-smart farming, paid out and repaid through M-Pesa.
- **USSD access:** farmers without internet dial `*483#` to get alerts and apply for loans from any phone.

## How it works

1. A hazard is detected and an alert goes out to farmers in the affected area.
2. A farmer dials `*483#` or uses the web app to view alerts and apply for a green loan.
3. The loan is disbursed via M-Pesa.
4. The farmer repays via M-Pesa.

## USSD menu

```
ResiliLink
1. Alerts for my area
2. Apply for a green loan
3. Repay via M-Pesa
4. Language
```
